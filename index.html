Math Olympiad Camp

One hour a day · Week 1 · Block A: Foundations & habits

The hour: 10 min warm-up → 20 min learning the concept → 25 min practice → 5 min error book. Mon–Thu are new topics, Friday is timed mixed review, Saturday is a full past paper, Sunday is off.

Put it on the iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPad (it must be Safari — Chrome on iOS cannot install).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. It appears as an app icon. Opening it gives a full screen with no browser bars.

After the first visit everything is cached, so it works on a plane, in the car, and with wifi off. Progress is stored on the iPad itself — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and there are no accounts, ads or trackers.

While you play

  • ⌨︎ Pad in the header switches between the built-in number pad and the iPad keyboard. The built-in pad keeps the keyboard from covering the question.
  • A− A+ ⤢ Fit resize everything; ⤢ Fit makes the text fill the screen you are on.
  • 🔊 mutes the sounds — useful in a classroom or a car.
